Third-party components used at runtime:

- JLayer 1.0.1 (javazoom:jlayer), GNU Lesser General Public License v2.1.
  Pure-Java MPEG audio decoder. Judio uses it for local and streamed MPEG
  Layers I, II, and III; its classes are neither modified nor shaded. The
  Maven POM lists JUnit only in test scope and no runtime dependencies. The
  LGPL-2.1 text is included as LICENSE-LGPL-2.1.txt.
- Concentus 1.0.2 (io.github.jaredmdobson:concentus), BSD-style Opus license.
  Pure-Java Opus decoder, including multistream support. Judio uses the
  decoder only; Concentus does not parse Ogg pages. Its full copyright,
  conditions, and disclaimer are reproduced in LICENSE-CONCENTUS.txt.
- JOrbis 0.0.17 (org.jcraft:jorbis), GNU Lesser General Public License v2.1.
  Pure-Java Vorbis decoder. The artifact includes JOrbis and Jogg classes and
  has no runtime dependencies. It is used through its packet-decoding API;
  Judio's Ogg container reader is separate. The LGPL-2.1 text is included as
  LICENSE-LGPL-2.1.txt.
- FlatLaf 3.7.2 (com.formdev:flatlaf), Apache License 2.0.
  Used only by the optional Swing demo player and not required by the library.

JLayer, Concentus, JOrbis, and FlatLaf remain separate dependencies and are
not shaded or modified in the published Judio jar. Their source/license links
and the codec architecture decision are documented in
docs/development/ogg-codec-architecture.md.

Test media and test-only TLS key:

- Ogg Opus/Vorbis tones and the MPEG Layer II tone are generated synthetic
  audio dedicated to CC0 1.0. The full CC0 text is in
  src/test/resources/audio/CC0-1.0.txt; see the two audio README files for
  provenance and reproduction commands.
- src/test/resources/audio/ogg/test-server.p12 is a self-signed localhost
  certificate and private key used only by HTTPS loopback tests. Its password
  is a test constant; it is not used by production code.
